# Kiwi Coach — Lean Canvas

*One-page summary for Stage 1 (Business Canvas requirement). Transfer into the supplied
Lean Canvas template / a one-page layout before submitting.*

---

**① Problem**
- Job seekers can follow all the standard interview advice and still not know whether their
  example proves fit for the specific role.
- Answers can be fluent and well-structured yet answer the wrong question (Auckland Eye feedback).
- Professional coaching costs NZ$150+/hour — 20/20 surveyed had been priced out.

*Existing alternatives:* generic ChatGPT prompting; Big Interview / Huru (delivery coaching);
Verve AI / Final Round AI (live in-interview assistance); university career appointments (limited hours).

**② Customer Segments**
- **End users:** international students & recent graduates seeking CS/IT roles in NZ (85% of survey).
- **Paying customers:** university career & international offices; private training providers
  (Dev Academy, Mission Ready).
- *Early adopters:* near-graduation international students in Auckland CS/IT programmes.

**③ Unique Value Proposition**
> Practise for the interview you're actually walking into. Kiwi Coach works out why a role
> exists, maps your CV evidence to it, and checks whether each answer was the *right* answer —
> before the interview, not during it.

*High-level concept:* role-fit coaching, not a generic question bank or a live cheating tool.

**④ Solution**
- Decode hiring intent from the JD + company context.
- Map CV evidence into a proof strategy (what proves fit, what to avoid).
- Adaptive text/voice practice grounded in that strategy.
- Report checking question-intent fit, evidence fit, and clarity — every claim traceable to
  what the candidate said.

**⑤ Channels**
- Student associations & job-seeker meetups (now, zero cost, exactly our segment).
- Private training providers — placement rate is their sales number (weeks to close).
- University career/international offices (slowest, largest).
- Employer referrals from hiring managers (a candidate-side tool they can recommend at no cost).

**⑥ Revenue Streams**
- Institutional cohort licences (primary) — per-cohort, moving to annual.
- Direct subscription NZ$19–29/month, plus pay-per-session ~NZ$5/30 min (40% preferred this).
- Price-tested: 95% likely to buy at NZ$19, 65% at NZ$29, 30% at NZ$39.

**⑦ Cost Structure**
- Variable: STT/TTS (82% of session cost), LLM inference. **Measured mean NZ$0.31/voice session.**
- Fixed: hosting, database/storage, monitoring, privacy & security.
- Development tooling (Codex/ChatGPT) — build cost, not per-user.

**⑧ Key Metrics**
- Report QA pass rate (post-recalibration).
- Session completion rate; sessions per user (repeat use).
- Pilot: usefulness rating, "generic feedback" complaint reduction, office funds a 2nd cohort.
- Gross margin per session (currently ~84% at NZ$19 / 10 sessions).

**⑨ Unfair Advantage**
- A data flywheel: schema for decoding hiring intent + a growing labelled set of
  question-and-answer alignments. The more sessions run, the better we know which evidence
  proves which role — a dataset a new entrant cannot buy.
- NZ-specific coaching knowledge (Tall Poppy, local hiring norms) global tools have no reason to build.
- Founder–market fit: we are the customer, and the core insight came from our own interview.

---

*Assumptions still to validate (highest first):* that a university will pay at all (untested —
only students have said they want it); that students complete sessions; that role-fit feedback
beats free generic AI. All three are the primary targets of the planned pilot.
